Demo sync experiment

Every night at midnight Europe/Madrid, the demo account is wiped and reseeded from drawlang@bohemiamarket.com. Signing in as demo/demo shows the current copy of that account's canvases. Anything anyone does inside the demo account is thrown away at midnight — that's why we can hand it out publicly without losing the curated showcase.

How it works

Source account (real, editable) → nightly copy job → demo account (wiped and reseeded).

Curating the showcase

The nightly job copies whatever lives on the source account — there is no fabricated seed.

  1. Sign out of admin. Sign in as the source account (email shown above).
  2. Build the showcase canvases you want visitors to see — data diode, AND-gate, FUP page, single-line diagram, P&ID, secure RTU, A3 frame, etc. Anything you’d be proud to show to a stranger.
  3. Sign back in as admin and hit Reset now below. The demo account is wiped and reseeded from the source in a single transaction.
  4. From that moment onwards, the same reset runs automatically every night at 00:00 Europe/Madrid.

Safety net: if the source account is empty for any reason, the reset falls back to a minimal hardcoded seed so demo/demo is never a dead login.
